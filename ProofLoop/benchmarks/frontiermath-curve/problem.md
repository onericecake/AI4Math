# FrontierMath public sample: points on a quartic curve

How many nonzero solutions of
\[
x^3y+y^3z+z^3x=0
\]
over \(\mathbb F_{5^{18}}\) are there up to scaling by a nonzero field element?

## Completion criteria

Give the exact integer and a derivation. Justify the curve's smoothness and genus (or use another complete method), supply reproducible exact counts or identities sufficient to determine the answer, and explain why they determine the count over \(\mathbb F_{5^{18}}\). State any unresolved gap. Do not use web search or execute code during the model run. You may include a short exact-arithmetic algorithm that can be executed by the evaluator afterward.
