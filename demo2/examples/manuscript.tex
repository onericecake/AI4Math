\title{A Small Extremal Result}
\begin{abstract}
We improve a bound for a family of graphs.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
The main theorem improves an earlier extremal estimate.
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Every graph in the stated family satisfies the improved bound.
\end{theorem}
\section{Conclusion}
The bound and its corollaries are proved.
