## Central result

Any polynomial $P(z^2)=\displaystyle{\sum^n_{k=0} a_k z^{2k}}$ with non-negative integer coefficients can be realized as the graded ruling polynomial of a Legendrian link $\La_{P(z^2)}$ in $\R^3_{std}$.

Let $P(z^2)=\displaystyle{\sum^n_{k=0} a_k z^{2k}}$ be a polynomial with non-negative coefficients. The augmentation variety of the Legendrian link $\La_{P(z^2)}$ is a disjoint union of $a_k$ copies of $(\C^*)^{2k}$ over all values of $k$.

Only the polynomials of the form $P(z^2)$ or $zP(z^2)$ can be realized as the ungraded ruling polynomial of a Legendrian link in $\R^3_{std}$.

Let $L_1, L_2$ be two smooth immersed ribbon fillings of $\La$ with double points that have U-compatible exteriors. Then, $L_1$ and $L_2$ are smoothly non-isotopic relative to their boundary.
