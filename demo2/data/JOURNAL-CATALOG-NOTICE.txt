The journal catalog fixtures were normalized from metadata made available by
zbMATH Open (https://zbmath.org/serials/ and its public API).

This snapshot was retrieved on 2026-09-01 and is provided for testing the
journal-matching prototype. Journal names, identifiers, URLs, and article
metadata remain subject to the source provider's terms and may change. Check
the current zbMATH Open API and legal terms before redistributing or using the
data in production:
https://zbmath.org/about/ and https://zbmath.org/static/zbmath.org/legal/api-terms.html

The catalog is not an endorsement of any journal and does not encode editorial
acceptance decisions.
